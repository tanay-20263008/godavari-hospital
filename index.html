About Us

Trusted Hospital
in Satpur, Nashik

Godavari Multispeciality Hospital & Critical Care is a trusted healthcare institution located in Satpur, Nashik, dedicated to providing high-quality, affordable, and patient-centered medical services. With a commitment to excellence, we offer comprehensive healthcare solutions across multiple specialties under one roof.

Our hospital is equipped with modern medical technology and advanced infrastructure. We take pride in our team of experienced doctors, skilled nursing staff, and dedicated healthcare professionals who work tirelessly to deliver compassionate care every day — 24 hours, 365 days a year.

At Godavari Multispeciality Hospital, Health, patient safety, comfort, and well-being are our top priorities. Our Critical Care Unit handles emergency and life-threatening conditions with prompt response and continuous monitoring.

Our Mission
High-quality healthcare at affordable cost, with full support for government schemes and cashless mediclaim — quality care for everyone.
Our Vision
To become Nashik's leading trusted hospital known for quality treatment, quick emergency services, and accessible healthcare for every section of society.

Hospital
Facilities

A 32-beded multispeciality hospital equipped with latest technology, advanced OT, ICU, and comprehensive diagnostics — all under one roof in Nashik.

32-Bed Multispeciality Hospital
Spacious facility with dedicated wards for general, surgical, paediatric, and critical care patients.
Modular Operation Theatre
Advanced modular OT with high-frequency C-Arm laparoscopy for precision surgeries across specialities.
ICU & Critical Care Unit
Fully equipped ICU with ventilators, central oxygen monitoring, defibrillators, 24 X 7 intensivist coverage.
Computerised Pathology Lab
Advanced computerised pathology laboratory with digital reporting for fast, accurate diagnostic results.
Digital X-Ray & Radiology
Digital X-ray system for clear, instant imaging with reduced radiation and faster reporting.
Deluxe, Special & General Rooms
Comfortable accommodation options for every patient's need and budget — Deluxe, Special, Semi-Special.
Well-Equipped Labour Room
Modern maternity ward with neonatal care for safe, comfortable deliveries around the clock.
24-Hour Ambulance Service
Round-the-clock ambulance with trained paramedics for rapid emergency response every day.
All Cashless Mediclaim
All government health schemes and private mediclaim accepted — stress-free, cashless treatment.
Cardiology & Heart Care

Post-Operative Monitoring
